A Major Change to Day Trading Rules in 2026 The Pattern Day Trader (PDT) rule has officially been replaced, marking one of the most significant changes to day trading regulations in decades. For active traders, swing traders, and self-directed investors, the elimination of the PDT rule removes a major barrier that historically limited trading flexibility for accounts under $25,000.
